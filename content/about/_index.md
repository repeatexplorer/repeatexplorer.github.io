RepeatExplorer is developed and maintained by the
[Laboratory of Molecular Cytogenetics](http://w3lamc.umbr.cas.cz/lamc/) at the
Institute of Plant Molecular Biology, Biology Centre of the Czech Academy of
Sciences, in České Budějovice.

The laboratory studies the sequence composition, molecular organisation and
evolution of plant genomes and chromosomes. Repetitive DNA is a central theme:
both transposable elements and satellite repeats, examined with experimental
methods alongside sequencing and the computational analysis these tools perform.
The group's own [publication list](http://w3lamc.umbr.cas.cz/lamc/?page_id=6)
and the [people who work on it](http://w3lamc.umbr.cas.cz/lamc/?page_id=10) are
on the laboratory site.

## The Galaxy server

The public [Galaxy server](/galaxy-server/) is part of the services provided by
[ELIXIR](https://www.elixir-czech.cz/), the European research infrastructure for
biological information. It is operated by [CESNET](https://www.cesnet.cz/) and
[CERIT-SC](https://www.cerit-sc.cz/).

If the server was used for published work, please add this statement:

> Computational resources for RepeatExplorer analysis were provided by the
> ELIXIR-CZ project (LM2023055), part of the international ELIXIR
> infrastructure.

This is separate from citing the tools themselves, which is covered on the
[publications page](/publications/).

## Contact

For help with the server, a larger data quota, or to report a problem, write to
the [server administrator](mailto:regalaxy@rt.cesnet.cz). If a tool fails while
running, report it with the bug icon in the dataset history, which sends the
job details along with the report.

Problems with a tool itself, rather than with the server, are best raised as an
issue in that tool's repository; every [tool page](/tools/) links to its own.

## Terms of use

RepeatExplorer is offered as a free, public, internet-accessible service, in the
hope that it is useful, but without any warranty.

Data transfer to the server is encrypted; data storage is not. If there are
restrictions on how your research data may be stored and used, consult your
institutional review board or the project's principal investigator before
uploading it. For protected data, large storage requirements or short deadlines,
run a local Galaxy instance with RepeatExplorer installed, or use the
command-line version of the tools.
